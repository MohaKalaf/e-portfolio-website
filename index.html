Computer Science student at Brunel. Building things that take ideas seriously.

I'm a second-year undergraduate at Brunel University London, looking for a placement year from July 2027. I spend most of my time outside coursework on things I want to exist — small tools, interactive demos, occasional larger systems where I get to think through a design problem end-to-end and write up the trade-offs honestly.

This summer I interned remotely at Safer Industries, building a role-based business simulation in Unity for their learning platform, where the same office scenario plays out differently as the CEO or the HR rep.

I'm drawn to projects where the interesting part isn't the technology but the design choices: what to include, what to cut, how to be honest about what you don't know. The work below is a slice of that.

Selected work

Work-Fit Matcher→ Apr 2026

Touch Symphony→ Apr 2026

An interactive playground that captures multi-touch position, pressure, velocity, and dwell data in real time, then visualises it as pressure-reactive trails, ripples, and a live heatmap. Includes a $1 gesture recognizer.
